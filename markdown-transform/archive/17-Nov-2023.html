Astronomers have discovered a planet called Wasp-107b, where it rains sand. This "candy floss" planet is known for its scorching temperatures, raging winds, and the delightful aroma of burnt matches. Not exactly a vacation destination, but hey, at least you won't have to worry about getting caught in the rain without an umbrella!
🌋 Earthtunes app lets you listen to Iceland's intensifying earthquake activity
Iceland is on high alert as seismic activity suggests a volcanic eruption is imminent. Scientists are using sonification to listen to the earthquakes, turning them into sounds like slamming doors or hail pelting a roof. It's like Mother Nature trying out her DJ skills. Watch out for her volcano remix!
