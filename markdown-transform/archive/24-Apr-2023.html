🧊 Motherhood on ice: lack of suitable men drives women to freeze their eggs
A new study by Yale professor Marcia C Inhorn has found that the biggest driving factor for women freezing their eggs was a shortage of suitable educated men rather than career or educational ambitions, with the trend often forced on successful high-earning women who have been looking for a partner but couldn’t find a reproductive partner due to the growing gap between men and women going into higher education.
🌡 Century-old Sydney weather record broken
Sydney has broken a century-old temperature record, with temperatures exceeding 20C for 184 consecutive days from 18 October 2016 to 19 April 2017, marking a new all-time high exceeding a 180-day period in 1913-14.
🤐 Google’s smart speakers are finally smart enough to shut up
Google's Nest smart speakers and displays will now chime instead of having a conversation when asked to control smart devices in the same room, in an effort to stop Google Assistant from chattering away unnecessarily.
🐭 Stanford researchers have engineered an organism to fight cancer (in mice)
Stanford researchers have developed a way to fight skin cancer in mice by engineering a friendly bacteria called Staphylococcus epidermidis to carry a tumor antigen, which triggered a potent immune reaction to wipe out cancer tumors in mice.
💰 We all contribute to AI – should we get paid for that?
Some experts propose the concept of "data dignity" in the age of AI, which means paying people for their contributions to AI systems and avoiding the current model of exchanging free services for data, but implementing this concept may face challenges due to difficulties in assigning appropriate amounts of credit and lack of access to data.
🚨 Emergency alert test UK: phone alarms sound at 3pm
UK mobile network Three is investigating why many of its users failed to receive an emergency alert from the government, the first nationwide test for the UK’s new national warnings system. The system is designed to warn the public if there is a danger to life nearby. However, while the piercing, 10-second tone arrived for millions of people, and up to a minute early for some, Three users reported en masse that they did not receive it.
