🏭 Cambridge develops floating factories for net-zero fuel
Cambridge University scientists have developed floating factories that can manufacture green fuel using sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide. These artificial leaves could potentially provide a net-zero fuel alternative for planes and ships, helping to reduce carbon emissions and combat climate change. Just imagine, fueling your car with a leaf!
