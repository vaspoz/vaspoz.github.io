🗼 Eiffel Tower may have inspired Van Gogh’s The Starry Night, says expert
Art historian James Hall believes that Vincent van Gogh's painting "The Starry Night" was inspired by the Eiffel Tower's spectacular opening, and that the swirling sky and clouds in the painting recall the Paris monument's display of pyrotechnics, electric light and explosions. Hall argues that for Van Gogh, the cypress tree depicted in the painting was a natural alternative to the Eiffel Tower, which he saw as an impressive example of French technological prowess.
