🤡 FTX's Cybersecurity Was Hilariously Bad
Crypto exchange FTX failed to protect customers' digital assets due to extremely poor cybersecurity practices, according to its latest bankruptcy report which shows the company never hired a chief information security officer and never put basic digital protections in place. The report also reveals FTX did not use cold storage, keeping virtually all clients' assets in hot wallets but claiming to investors it used cold storage. FTX also kept clients' sensitive cryptographic keys and seed phrases stored in plaintext documents and failed to enforce basic web security measures like multi-factor authentication.
