🏆 EV smashed the world record for distance on a single charge
Move over, Swiss student engineers! The Technical University of Munich has set a new record for the longest distance traveled on a single charge by an electric vehicle. Clocking in at 1,599 miles, this EV is 25 times more efficient than any EV on sale. That's one way to avoid stopping at the dreaded charging station on a road trip! But with a top speed of just 26 mph, you might need a lifetime supply of patience to reach your destination. The muc22, as it's called, might not be breaking any speed records, but it sure knows how to go the distance. Can't wait to see what other student-set world records are in store for us!
🚀 NASA’s Osiris-Rex capsule lands in Utah with Bennu asteroid sample
After a 1.2 billion-mile journey, the sample capsule from NASA's Osiris-Rex mission has successfully landed in the Utah desert. The scientists were so excited that they even broke into tears, but don't worry, they had plenty of tissues on hand to wipe away their scientific tears. Now the samples will be studied to uncover the mysteries of the universe and possibly make some new interstellar friends. Just remember, if you find any alien life in there, make sure to introduce them to Queen's Sir Brian May, he'll be immensely proud.

🗺 Google sued over fatal Google Maps error after man drove off broken bridge
Google is being sued over a fatal Google Maps error, where a man drove off a broken bridge. Apparently, the bridge had been labeled the "Bridge to Nowhere," but Google Maps insisted it was a "Bridge to Adventure." Well, they certainly delivered on the adventure part.

👨 GitHub CEO: Despite AI, demand for software developers still outweigh supply
GitHub CEO Thomas Dohmke believes that despite the rise of AI in software development, the demand for human developers will still outweigh the supply. With the growing amount of code, legacy systems, and the need to transform outdated languages like COBOL, developers will continue to be in high demand. As Dohmke says, "Every company is now a software company, except my great-uncle's pickle farm, but that's a different story." #DevelopmentDilemmas #CodeCrunch
