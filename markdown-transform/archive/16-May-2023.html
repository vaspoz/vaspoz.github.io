🍔 Why we need a new definition of junk food
Ultra-processed foods, which are now estimated to make up 60% of our diet, are causing extensive health problems and should be included in national nutrition guidance alongside warnings about salt, fat, and sugar, according to University College London’s Chris van Tulleken. In an article for The Guardian, van Tulleken argued that UPFs pose health risks beyond just the nutritional content they provide, due to the way they are processed, and suggested that public policy changes should focus on improving access to healthier alternatives rather than stigmatising disadvantaged people who rely on cheap, ultra-processed products.
💣 Two students built an A-bomb (2003)
Two physics students, Dave Dobson and Bob Selden, enlisted in a covert Pentagon operation known as the Nth Country Project to answer the question: how easy would it be for a country with no nuclear expertise to build a nuclear bomb? The two men, with no nuclear expertise but both having physics PhDs, were tasked with cracking the "nuclear secret" without access to classified research. Their success in designing an atom bomb in just 30 months 40 years ago reveals that terrorists could easily create a nuclear bomb today if they had access to the right materials.
