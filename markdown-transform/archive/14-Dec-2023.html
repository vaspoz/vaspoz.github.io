🧬 FDA approves first CRISPR therapy–here's how it works against sickle cell
The FDA has approved the first CRISPR therapy to treat sickle cell disease, a condition that affects around 100,000 people in the US. The therapy turns on the production of fetal hemoglobin, preventing the deformation of red blood cells. Finally, sickle cell disease doesn't stand a chance against genetic editing!

Now, Docker can ensure their containers don't just look pretty but also perform well in the real world.

👻 Dropbox spooks users by sending data to OpenAI for AI search features
Dropbox had its users in a "data consent scare" when it was discovered that their data was being shared with OpenAI for an AI search feature. Dropbox assures users that data is only shared if the feature is actively being used and is deleted within 30 days. Phew, crisis averted!
