🐒 Fate of 1k trafficked lab monkeys at center of US investigation in limbo
Over 1,000 Cambodian long-tailed macaques trafficked to a primate centre in Houston, Texas, are at risk of being killed or resold if they are returned to Cambodia or kept in the lab, according to animal welfare groups. Talks between the US Department of Justice, animal rights group PETA and sanctuary group BornFreeUSA stalled last week when PETA discovered the monkeys were to be flown out of the country, leaving only three options: the animals could be removed to a sanctuary; they may be killed; or they could be re-exported. Charles River Laboratories, which owns the centre, is currently blocked from testing or selling the animals until it confirms to the DoJ that they are captive bred.
