💸 Britons ‘need to accept’ they’re poorer, says Bank of England economist
The Bank of England's chief economist, Huw Pill, has said that UK households and businesses need to accept they are poorer and stop seeking pay increases and pushing prices higher in response to rising costs, which is resulting in a game of "pass the parcel" in the economy. Pill warned that someone needs to accept that they're worse off and stop trying to maintain their real spending power by bidding up prices or passing energy costs through to customers, adding that the reluctance to accept lower living standards is generating inflation in the UK.

🔄 Intuit’s Shift
Intuit, the US financial and accounting software company, is shifting its operations to become an AI-driven company, with CEO Sasan Goodarzi leading the charge; analysts suggest this could help the company leverage its vast customer base and talent pool into more efficient growth. Intuit's bet on AI technology has faced some growing pains including layoffs and revenue challenges but the company believes that through its "AI-driven expert platform" strategy, it can offer more value to customers by reducing work and increasing revenue. The company's strategy will focus on using AI and machine learning to augment its existing platforms in personal finance, accounting, and income tax returns. The second-largest team of Intuit is in India, where the company is developing an AI model for the mid-market, which is showing positive results using templates that will soon be converted to generative AI.

🔒 Google’s New Two-Factor Authentication Isn’t End-to-End Encrypted, Tests Show
Google's new two-factor authentication tool, Authenticator, has been found to pose significant security risks as it is not end-to-end encrypted, allowing Google to access user secrets stored on their servers and increasing the risk for cyber attacks and data breaches. Developers and researchers who discovered the issue have urged users to avoid the new syncing feature in the app to preserve their privacy. Google has acknowledged the problem and plans to offer end-to-end encryption in Google Authenticator in the future.
