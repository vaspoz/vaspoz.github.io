Rate Limiting is like telling someone, "Whoa, slow down on the API calls!" It helps prevent system overload, DDoS attacks, and brute force attempts. There are 4 main algorithms: Fixed-window (simple but can cause bursts), Sliding-window (fair but more complex), Leaky Bucket (steady flow), and Token Bucket (allows bursts). Choose wisely!
