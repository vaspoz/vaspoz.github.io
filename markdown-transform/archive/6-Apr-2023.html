🧠 False Memories Can Form Within Seconds, Study Finds
A new study has found that people can develop false memories of an event, even just seconds after it happens, and that such misremembering is influenced by expectations and preconceived notions. While research on memory often focuses on long-term memories, the study looked at short-term memory and found that volunteers had difficulty remembering whether certain letters were mirrored or reversed. The findings suggest that our memory can be distorted from the moment memories are formed, based on our preconceptions of what "should" happen.
