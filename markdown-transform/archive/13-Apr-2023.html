🚫 Google will shut down Dropcam and Nest Secure in 2024
Google will be shutting down support for Dropcam and Nest Secure home security systems by April 8th, 2024, giving one year's notice as it prepares to integrate more products into the Google Home app. Nest Protect smart smoke alarms are the only remaining app-only Nest devices, and Google has promised to bring them to the new Google Home app. WorkswithNest connections will also be shut down by September 29th, 2023. Eligible Nest Secure customers will be contacted with an exclusive offer for a next-generation security system from ADT or $200 to use on the Google Store.
