💸 Hackers drain Bitcoin ATMs of $1.5M by exploiting 0-day bug
Hackers have drained over $1.5 million from cryptocurrency ATMs by exploiting a zero-day vulnerability, leaving customers responsible for unrecoverable losses, according to ATM manufacturer General Bytes, which has issued an apology and is reviewing its security procedures. To pull off the heist, an unknown actor exploited a previously unknown vulnerability, allowing the attacker to upload and execute a malicious Java application. Hot wallets remain a weak link in cryptocurrency security, as evidenced by the risks posed to internet-connected wallets like ATM hot wallets.
