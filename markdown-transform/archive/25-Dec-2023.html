🏦 Corvids seem to handle temporary memories the way we do
Move over humans, it turns out birds like corvids have a similar working memory as us. They categorize information using attractor dynamics, which explains why they remember the general concept of a color (like "red") but not the specific shade. Looks like birds have a lot more than just feathers to boast about!
🚗 7.1M miles, 3 minor injuries: Waymo's safety data looks good
Waymo's safety data is impressive! Their driverless cars have only caused three minor injuries in 7.1 million miles of operation. In comparison, human drivers would have caused about 13 injuries in the same mileage. Looks like the machines are giving humans a run for their money...or perhaps a run without injuries!

☔ Pakistan uses artificial rain in attempt to cut pollution levels
In an effort to combat air pollution, Pakistan attempted to lower pollution levels in Lahore by using artificial rain. They used cloud seeding to create rain, but experts warn it's not a sustainable solution. Maybe they should try cloud seeding with soap bubbles instead, that way they can have rain and a bubble party at the same time!
🔓 SSH protects the most sensitive networks. It just got a lot weaker
Researchers have discovered a new hack called Terrapin that could potentially undermine the cryptographic protections of SSH. This attack works when an attacker has a man-in-the-middle position on the connection, and it targets the SSH data stream during the handshake. Looks like SSH just got a little less secure. Time to start teleporting data instead!
🦌 'Zombie deer disease' epidemic spreads, scientists fear it may jump to humans
"Zombie deer disease" is spreading in Yellowstone National Park, with fears it may jump to humans. The disease causes abnormal behavior in cervids like deer and elk, leaving them drooling, lethargic, and with a "blank stare." Looks like it's not just humans who get that way after a long day at work!
