💰 People Put Nearly $1B into Apple Savings Accounts in First 4 Days
Apple has reportedly attracted deposits of close to $1bn in just four days after its new high-yield savings account was launched, according to anonymous sources cited by Forbes. The bank is backed by Goldman Sachs and has an interest rate of 4.15%, compared to the national average rate of 0.24%, though analysts are debating whether the bank's success is due to user loyalty to the company, the proliferation of iPhones or interest rates rising elsewhere. The account is restricted to those with iPhones using the latest iOS 12.4 or later, and an associated iCloud account "in good standing".
