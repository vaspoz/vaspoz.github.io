🔭 Astrophysicists Spot Einstein Rings That Boost Case for Axionic Dark Matter
Researchers studying lensed ‘Einstein rings’ of light have found that signatures in the rings indicate the kind of dark matter that inhabits the background galaxies, revealing the presence of wave-like dark matter, according to a study published in Nature Astronomy. This could make the axion, a hypothetical, small boson particle, a more likely candidate for dark matter, instead of Weakly Interacting Massive Particles (WIMPs), which has been theorised to only interact just enough with ordinary matter to make them invisible. However, more observations and modeling must be done to better understand the nature of the dark matter.
