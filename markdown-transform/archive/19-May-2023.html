🎨 Bruno Schulz: An Artist, a Murder, and the Hijacking of History
Benjamin Balint's new biographical study explores the fascinating life and afterlife of Jewish writer and artist, Bruno Schulz, who attracted a smaller but more passionately devoted group of readers, including Philip Roth, Cynthia Ozick and Nicole Krauss, propelling Schulz to be the quintessential writer’s writer, but ultimately leading to his tragic death in 1942. This review considers the events that made Schulz's contemporary reputation inseparable from the hijacking of his literary and artistic legacy and addresses the debates surrounding the ownership of his murals, his place of origin and the absurdity of the modern nation state.
