💬 AI gains “values” with Anthropic’s new Constitutional AI chatbot approach

🦠 Microbes discovered that can digest plastics at low temperatures
Scientists have found microbes that can digest plastics at temperatures as low as 15C, offering potential for breakthroughs in microbial recycling, as previously discovered microbes can typically only work at temperatures above 30C, which makes industrial practice prohibitively expensive.
🔬 Qubits 30 meters apart used to confirm Einstein was wrong about quantum
An experiment using qubits 30 meters apart has demonstrated that Einstein was wrong about local realism in quantum mechanics, with the experiment being the first to separate qubits by enough distance to ensure that light isn't fast enough to travel between them while measurements are made, potentially aiding future quantum computing efforts.
