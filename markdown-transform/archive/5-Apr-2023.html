🔑 Nexx garage door controllers use a universal password
A leading garage door controller has been found to have severe security and privacy vulnerabilities that can allow anyone with a moderate technical background to search Nexx servers for a given email address, device ID or name and then issue commands to the associated controller, without the need for authorisation, impacting over 40,000 devices located in residential and commercial properties, leading the researcher who discovered the flaw to advise anyone using one of the controllers to immediately disconnect it until the vulnerabilities are fixed.
