💡 Physicists Designed an Experiment to Turn Light into Matter
Physicists are using simulations to show that plasma collisions could yield matter by harnessing Einstein's E = mc^2 equation. It's like turning photons into a delightful electron-positron pair. Talk about a laser show! Who knew lasers could not only be cool, but also scientifically productive?
🐭 3D-printed chip showing body's reaction to drugs could end need for animal tests
Scientists have created a 3D-printed "body-on-chip" device that could potentially replace animal testing in drug development. The device mimics the flow of medicine through different organs, allowing researchers to test drug reactions without using live animals. Finally, a way to save animals and watch TV at the same time!
🔓 4-year campaign backdoored iPhones using advanced exploit
Researchers have discovered a sophisticated four-year campaign that backdoored iPhones, infecting devices belonging to employees of Kaspersky. The attackers exploited an undocumented hardware feature, proving that even iPhones are vulnerable to sneaky tactics. Looks like Kaspersky got Kasper-SNEAKed! Talk about a covert operation!
💉 Injection of "smart insulin" regulates blood glucose levels for one week
Researchers have developed a "smart insulin" that can regulate blood glucose levels for up to a week in animals. Finally, a way for diabetics to have their cake and eat it too (as long as they inject themselves with smart insulin first)!
🔬 Researchers Test 2,400-Year-Old Leather and Realize It's Made of Human Skin
Researchers have discovered that Scythians in Ukraine used human skin to make leather, confirming Herodotus' claim. Talk about a macabre fashion statement! Imagine showing off your quiver, only for someone to say, "Nice leather, is that human?" Guess those Scythians were really into DIY projects.
🐜 Getting to the bottom of how red flour beetles absorb water through their butts
Scientists have discovered that red flour beetles can absorb water through their butts. Talk about a creative way to hydrate! It turns out their rectum is a super-efficient water-harvesting system. I guess you could say these beetles really know how to "get to the bottom" of hydration!
