🦍 Gene therapy reverses vision loss in primates – by making their eyes young again
A new gene therapy has been developed by Harvard Medical School and Life Biosciences that reversed vision loss in primates caused by non-arteritic anterior ischemic optic neuropathy (NAION) by making their eye cells "young" again, potentially signaling a new era in the treatment of age-related diseases.
🚇 Boring Company Will Expand Las Vegas Tunnels to 65-Mile Network
Las Vegas officials have approved The Boring Company's plans to expand their network of underground tunnels, resulting in 69 stations and 65 miles of tunnels, with executive director of infrastructure Mike Janssen commenting on the funding project, provided by Boring Company, and the system's reduction of traffic, alternative transportation, and revenue sharing agreement with the company.
💰 TikTok opens up its revamped creator fund to all eligible creators in the US
TikTok has launched its revamped creator fund, called the "Creativity Program Beta," to all eligible creators in the United States, with the aim of generating higher revenue and unlocking more real-world opportunities for creators, and to start earning with the program, creators will need to create high-quality, original videos that are longer than one minute.
💊 New Alzheimer’s drug slows cognitive decline by 35%, trial results show
Pharmaceutical company Lilly is applying for approval for its drug donanemab in the US next month, after the drug slowed cognitive decline by up to 36% in Alzheimer's patients with early-stage symptoms in late-stage trials, marking the second Alzheimer's drug in a year to succeed in trials and prompting scientists to suggest it could be "the beginning of the end" for the disease. However, the drug also had side-effects, including brain swelling and bleeding.
💻 Ransomware attack forces Dallas to shut down courts, disrupts some 911 services

💼 Shopify to reduce workforce by 20%, sells logistics biz to Flexport for equity
Shopify is reducing its workforce by 20% and selling its logistics business to Flexport for 13% equity, as the company focuses on its core e-commerce software and prepares for the AI revolution.
🖱 Microsoft’s mice, keyboards, and webcams are being discontinued
Microsoft is discontinuing its line of Microsoft-branded mice, keyboards, and webcams in favor of focusing on its Surface-branded PC accessories.
🤖 AI ‘could be as transformative as Industrial Revolution’
Sir Patrick Vallance, the UK's outgoing chief scientific adviser, has warned MPs that there will be a "big impact on jobs" from generative AI, urging the government to get ahead of its potentially "transformative" impact on society and the economy. Sir Patrick called for a national review of which sectors would be most affected, stating that AI could replace jobs, leading to either mass unemployment or the creation of work only humans could perform. IBM announced this week that it is suspending or reducing hiring in roles such as human resources, with a suggestion that 30% of back-office roles could be replaced by AI in five years.
💬 Salesforce Announces SlackGPT
