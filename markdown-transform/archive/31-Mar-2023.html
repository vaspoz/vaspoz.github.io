🌱 Plants emit ultrasonic sounds in rapid bursts when stressed, scientists say
Scientists have recorded ultrasonic popping sounds from thirst or damaged plants emitting up to 50 staccato pops an hour, suggesting that the plant kingdom isn’t as silent as it seems, and opening the possibility that such sounds could be interpreted by other organisms to shape ecosystems or influence the behaviour of animals such as moths and mice. The sounds, which are emitted in rapid bursts, were found to be emitted more frequently when the plants were deprived of water or had their stems cut, and could potentially be detected at a distance of three to five metres.
