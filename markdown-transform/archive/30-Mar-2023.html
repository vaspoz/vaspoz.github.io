🛑 Top Technologists and Elon Musk Demand Immediate Pause of Advanced AI Systems
A coalition of over 500 technologists, engineers, and AI ethicists, including Apple co-founder Steve Wozniak and “based AI” developer Elon Musk, have signed an open letter calling on AI labs to pause training on any AI systems more powerful than Open AI’s recently released GPT-4 for at least six months, citing potential risks to society and humanity. The letter argues that emerging AI systems currently lack meaningful safeguards or controls and calls on AI labs to implement and agree on a shared set of safety protocols to ensure systems are audited by independent experts. It also asks for policymakers to develop robust AI governance systems and watermarking tools to help distinguish between synthetic and real content.
🐰 Open source espresso machine is one delicious rabbit hole inside another
A retired programmer and technical writer, Norm Sohl, has built a highly configurable espresso machine using open-source hardware plans and the Gaggiuino software project, salvaging parts from an Espresso Gaggia and building a chassis out of extrusion rails and stiffening plates - the machine is still a work in progress, but Sohl hopes future versions will make use of Gaggiuino's own circuit board design.
💰 Orb, which helps B2B companies price their products, raises $19.1M

💔 Disney cuts metaverse division as part of broader restructuring
Disney has eliminated its metaverse division as part of company-wide staff cuts that aim to reduce headcount by around 7,000 over the next two months, which included the next-generation storytelling and consumer experiences unit that was developing metaverse strategies.
💻 $39 Cooler Master case turns your old Framework Laptop parts into a tiny PC
Cooler Master has partnered with modular laptop maker Framework to produce a new $39 case that can turn old Framework mainboards into a desktop PC, complete with four expansion card slots for a range of additional storage, USB-A, USB-C, ethernet, HDMI, DisplayPort and microSD ports.
👍 Twitter to no longer only promote paid-for accounts after backlash
Twitter has reversed its decision to limit visibility on the "for you" timeline to only paying users after receiving backlash, with CEO Elon Musk claiming he "forgot to mention" that other users would also be visible on the tab.
🎩 Mathematicians Find New 13-Sided Shape Called 'The Hat'
Mathematicians have discovered a theoretical, 13-sided shape called "the hat" that can tile a surface without repeating, marking a breakthrough in aperiodic tiling, according to a research team led by mathematician David Smith and colleagues that proved its existence in a preprint paper posted online this month.
💻 Google’s AlloyDB Omni lets you run its PostgreSQL-compatible DB anywhere
Google Cloud has launched AlloyDB Omni, a downloadable edition of its fully managed cloud-based database service that can be installed on premises, at the edge, or on laptops, which is twice as fast for transactional workloads and can deliver 100 times faster analytical queries than the standard open-source version of PostgreSQL, allowing Google's customers to modernize their workloads faster than they can move to the cloud.
🕵 Twitter obtains subpoena forcing GitHub to unmask source-code leaker
Twitter has obtained a subpoena that forces GitHub to unmask the user who leaked portions of Twitter's source code and must provide identifying information by April 3rd.
🚫 GitHub takes down repository containing Twitter’s source code
GitHub takes down a repository containing Twitter's source code after the social network filed a DMCA takedown request; the move comes ahead of Elon Musk supposedly open-sourcing Twitter's algorithm used to recommend tweets on March 31, and it is not clear what part of Twitter's source code was leaked on GitHub or for how long.
