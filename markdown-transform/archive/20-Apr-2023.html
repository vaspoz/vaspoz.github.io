🌋 Volcanic microbe eats CO2 ‘astonishingly quickly’, say scientists
Scientists have discovered a new microbe that absorbs carbon dioxide from the atmosphere at an "astonishingly quick" rate, potentially providing an efficient way of capturing the greenhouse gas. The cyanobacteria was found in volcanic seeps near the Italian island of Vulcano, where water contains high levels of CO2. It converts CO2 into biomass faster than any other known cyanobacteria. The microbe also sinks in water, which means it could be collected alongside the CO2 it absorbs. However, the team behind the research, which was funded by biotech company Seed Health, cautioned that the microbe was not a "silver bullet" solution to climate change.
