🍻 Study: Scotland’s minimum pricing linked to 13% drop in alcohol-related deaths
Scotland's minimum pricing for alcohol, which imposes a minimum price of 50p per unit, has been associated with a 13% drop in the number of deaths from alcohol consumption and a reduction of 411 hospitalisations per year on average, according to a study published in The Lancet. Researchers from Public Health Scotland, the University of Glasgow and the University of Queensland, Australia found the largest reduction in mortality rates for males and those living in the 40% most deprived areas. The policy was introduced in May 2018.

💊 Gut feelings: why drugs that nurture your microbes; the future of mental health
Researchers are exploring the potential of psychobiotic drugs that can alter the composition of microbes in the gut, to treat mental health issues such as depression and anxiety. The gut is known to influence the brain, and researchers believe that by administering certain microbes or nurturing beneficial bacterial growth, disorders related to the brain could be combated. Researchers have discovered that taking probiotics can lower negative mood and shifting the microbe composition in animals can reduce stress, anxiety and depression. Prebiotics, such as vegetables and nuts, and cutting processed foods from diets have all been shown to aid microbiome health.
