📚 Tumblr's owner is striking deals with OpenAI and Midjourney for training data
Tumblr's owner is reportedly striking deals with AI companies for training data, marking an interesting convergence of technology and creativity. It seems like Tumblr is taking inspiration from its users' posts to train AI, which is perfect for those times when you need an algorithm's take on a cryptic Tumblr post.
🧪 Breakdown products of niacin may spur vascular inflammation
There's a surprising link between niacin and the risk of heart attack and stroke. Who knew that something as innocent as Vitamin B3 could be causing cardiovascular issues? I guess sometimes you just can't trust those pesky vitamins. Watch out for those fortified cereals and breads, they may not be as heart-healthy as you thought!
🔬 Microplastics found in every human placenta tested in study
Well, it seems like microplastics are the unwelcome guests at the party in our bodies. They've managed to find their way into human placentas and even clog up our blood vessels. I guess our bodies are just trying to keep up with the latest trends in pollution. Time to RSVP with some serious recycling efforts, folks!
🚀 SpaceX discloses cause of Starship anomalies as it clears an FAA hurdle
It seems SpaceX had a bit of a rough time with their Starship, with "rapid unscheduled disassembly" being a fancy way of saying it exploded. They identified the cause as a blockage in the liquid oxygen filter, which sounds serious, but at least it wasn't a clogged toilet.
❄ Restoring Arctic sea ice by pumping water to the surface
A Dutch startup is proposing to use the same technique used to create outdoor skating rinks to help save the melting Arctic ice. By pumping water onto the ice, they aim to thicken it and increase its ability to reflect the sun's rays. Who knew ice-skating could be so environmentally friendly?
🚆 Electric cars produce twice as much CO₂ as trains, says rail group data
Did you know that certain train journeys in Britain produce one-fifteenth of the CO2 emissions per person compared to driving in a petrol or diesel car? I guess you could say that trains are on the right track when it comes to being eco-friendly. Choo-choo to the planet's rescue! 🚂🌍
💼 GitHub's Copilot Enterprise is now generally available at $39 a month
GitHub has announced the general availability of Copilot Enterprise, their code completion tool and chatbot for businesses. With the ability to reference internal code and knowledge base, Copilot can now even help with non-engineering questions like vacation policies. Soon, it may be asking Copilot for dating advice.
🤖 How would an AI turn out if you raised it like a child?
Researchers at New York University attempted to train an AI using a database of video footage taken from a camera strapped to a baby named Sam. The AI learned to recognize objects but struggled with understanding context and movement. Baby steps for AI, but still a long way to go!
🔬 Solar storms, ice cores and nuns' teeth: the new science of history
Advances in scientific techniques like spectrometry and gene sequencing are revolutionizing history as we know it. Now historians can uncover the truth about ancient civilizations through physical remains and forensic analysis. Finally, we can solve the mysteries of the past without resorting to time travel or consulting ancient oracles!
💨 Carbon capture startup takes 'good enough' approach to slash costs
Clairity Technology, a carbon capture startup, is taking a more relaxed approach to capturing CO2. They aim for 80% purity, avoiding the expensive pursuit of 99.9% purity. Their "good enough" method saves costs and allows for the use of cheaper sorbent materials like baking soda. Maybe they're onto something!
