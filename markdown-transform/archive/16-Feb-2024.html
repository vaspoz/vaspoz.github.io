💊 Drug offers 'breakthrough' in treatment of asbestos-linked cancer
Scientists have developed a new drug to treat mesothelioma, a notoriously hard-to-treat cancer linked to asbestos. In an international trial, the drug quadrupled three-year survival rates. Finally, some good news for asbestos - its best friend cancer is getting a bit easier to beat!
🧪 Lab-grown 'beef rice' could offer more sustainable protein source, say creators

The rice has a pale pink hue and a "pleasant and novel flavour experience," according to the researchers. Looks like the future of food is... pink!
