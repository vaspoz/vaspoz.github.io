🦆 DuckDuckGo's browser adds encrypted, privacy-minded syncing and backup
DuckDuckGo continues to add privacy features to its browser, including encrypted syncing. No sign-in or account needed, and they promise they don't see what you're syncing. Finally, a browser that won't judge your bookmark collection! Your secret love for cat memes is safe with DuckDuckGo.
💔 Broadcom-owned VMware kills the free version of ESXi virtualization software
VMware's decision to kill the free version of its ESXi virtualization software is a blow to tinkerers and hobbyists. Looks like Broadcom is taking the "no such thing as a free lunch" motto seriously. Well, at least we still have dad's old dual-booted Windows/Linux system to play with.
💻 US Military notifies 20k of data breach after cloud email leak
The US military is getting all sentimental by sharing personal information with the world, thanks to a cloud email leak. It turns out they wanted to give everyone a taste of what it feels like to be part of their top-secret operations. How thoughtful!
🤖 US says AI models can't hold patents
The USPTO has made it clear that only human inventors can hold patents, leaving AI inventors feeling dismayed. Looks like the Terminator won't be coming up with any brilliant ideas anytime soon. On the bright side, at least Arnold Schwarzenegger can breathe a sigh of relief.
🤖 Waymo recalls robotaxi software after two cars crashed into the same towed truck
Waymo recalls robotaxi software after cars crash into tow truck. Waymo's motto: "If you can't beat 'em, crash into 'em!" Jokes aside, it's good to see they're taking safety seriously. Now they just need to work on predicting the future motion of improperly towed vehicles.

💔 Zerodha. Uncategorized
A failure of the primary leased line to a CTCL between a stock broker and a stock exchange led to the activation of a backup leased line that was operating sporadically over the following hour, affecting bracket and cover orders. Subsequently, the process of placing and validating orders had been modified to incorporate the unreliability of the CTCL's leased lines, but the reliability of the primary and the backup leased lines was not fundamentally improved by the providers.
