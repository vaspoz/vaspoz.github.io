Scientists at Johnson Space Center are "overjoyed" to have access to samples from the 4.6-billion-year-old asteroid Bennu. Hopefully, they won't find any interstellar dust bunnies that have been hiding in there for millennia.
🕰 Dave Mills, inventor of NTP, dies at 85
Inventor of NTP, the protocol that keeps time on billions of devices, passes away at 85. His synchronized timekeeping system was so accurate, he could even tell you when the punchline of a joke was due. Rest in peace, Dr. Mills, you kept the Internet punctual and our dad jokes on time.
🧟 Arctic zombie viruses in Siberia could spark terrifying new pandemic, scientists
Scientists warn that ancient viruses, trapped in Arctic permafrost for millennia, could be unleashed by the warming climate, potentially causing a major disease outbreak. These Methuselah microbes, also known as zombie viruses, have researchers concerned about a new global medical emergency. Looks like even viruses have a chilling comeback.
