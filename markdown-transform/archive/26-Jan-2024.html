⏰ Inventor of NTP protocol that keeps time on billions of devices dies at age 85
Dr. David L. Mills, the inventor of Network Time Protocol (NTP), has passed away at age 85. His synchronized timekeeping system became the backbone of the modern digital infrastructure, ensuring accurate timestamps for network activities. Now he's off to synchronize celestial parties in the great beyond.
