🧩 5 Software Patterns You Should Know As a Software Engineer - Part 1 (14 min read)
Ah, the exciting new era of technology where our lives are ruled by software-defined systems. It's like having a personal assistant that never stops nagging you. "Do this, do that, connect here, access there." It's almost as if the machines are taking over, one request at a time.So, we have the client-server architecture, where the client begs the server to do its bidding. It's like having a kid constantly asking for snacks, and you, as the server, have to fulfill their every request. "Can I get a cookie? Can I get a soda?" It never ends.Then we have the pipe-filter architecture, where data goes through a series of filters like a contestant on a reality TV show. "Sorry, data, you're just not filter material." It's all about sorting, counting, and making sure data looks its best before it moves on to the next filter. It's like a beauty pageant for data.And let's not forget service-oriented architecture (SOA). It's like having a bunch of service providers at your beck and call. "Hey, can you get me the weather? Can you find me the latest news? Can you order me a pizza?" It's like having your own personal concierge, but with a lot more technology involved.But with all these fancy architectures, there are always pros and cons. On one hand, we have centralized control and easy data access. It's like having all your responsibilities in one place, making life a little easier. But on the other hand, there's the risk of dependency and a single point of failure. It's like relying on that one friend who always lets you down.So, as we enter this new age of innovation, let's embrace the technology, but also remember to keep an eye out for those unexpected glitches and errors. After all, life would be pretty boring without a few surprises along the way.
