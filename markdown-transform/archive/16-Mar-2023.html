🥗 Mediterranean diet may lower dementia risk by a quarter, study suggests
A Mediterranean diet, rich in fruits, vegetables, whole grains, and healthy fats, could help reduce the risk of dementia by almost a quarter, according to research conducted in the UK using data from more than 60,000 individuals. The benefits were found to apply regardless of a person’s genetic risk for the condition. The findings could help pave the way for new preventive treatments, although the study’s authors have noted that further research will be required to confirm the results.
💪 South Korea U-turns on 69-hour working week after youth backlash
South Korea's planned raise in its maximum weekly working hours to 69 has been put on hold after protests from millennials and generation Z who claimed that the move would damage their work-life balance and risk their health. President Yoon Suk-yeol has ordered government agencies to reconsider the measure and communicate better with the public, particularly with younger people. The proposed increase followed complaints from business groups that the current limit of 52 hours was hampering their ability to meet deadlines. Unions argued that the increase would simply force people to work longer hours.
🌜 The battle to boost our deep sleep – and help stop dementia
Scientists are homing in on ways to boost the brain's cleaning system during deep sleep, which could ultimately help keep our brains healthier for longer and reduce the risk of dementia and other brain diseases. The glymphatic system is the biological "brain washing" function of sleep, which filters out toxins and is important for brain health. Scientists are investigating ways to optimise and reboot deep sleep as we age. The key is to focus on sleep quality, which means avoiding coffee, alcohol, exercise and electronic devices in the run-up to bed and maintaining a dark bedroom overnight.
