💸 Twitch Turbo is going from $8.99 per month to $11.99 per month in the US
Twitch is following in the footsteps of many streaming services by raising the price of their ad-free subscription. Formerly $8.99, Twitch Turbo will now cost $11.99 per month. Looks like gamers will have to decide whether a Turbo boost is worth the extra cash.
🚀 At long last, the glorious future we were promised in space is on the way
NASA awards Blue Origin $3.4 billion to build reusable lunar lander. Blue Origin owner Jeff Bezos has finally won a coveted piece of NASA's Artemis program. The lander will be reusable and go up and down to the moon, serviced by a transport vehicle that will be fueled in low-Earth orbit.

💿 Alan Wake 2 and the death of disc-based video games
Looks like video games on CDs are going the way of the dodo, as big-name publishers Remedy and Epic announce no plans to release Alan Wake 2 on disc. A digital-only future could be in store for console gaming.
💔 A comprehensive list of 2023 tech layoffs

🤖 Sam Altman Says OpenAI Will Leave the EU If There's Any Real AI Regulation
OpenAI CEO Sam Altman is taking his AI pitch to Europe, promoting ChatGPT and AI regulatory policies. Concerned about the EU's "high-risk" designation, Altman said compliance could be difficult. OpenAI has faced more scrutiny in the EU than in the US. Possible regulation benefits the company but could limit smaller companies.
🚀 Arc’s new Boosts feature lets you change the way any website looks
Arc's new Boosts feature gives users control over the way websites look and work, allowing them to change colors and fonts or hide unwanted parts of the page. Just don't go too crazy, or you might make things worse. This new version of an old idea puts the power in users' hands.
🤑 OpenAI CEO raises $115M for crypto company that scans people’s eyeballs
Worldcoin's Orb is giving new meaning to "looking" at your investments. But don't worry, it's not a dystopian nightmare, just a way to establish your unique personhood and make sure you're not a bot. Plus, you get some free bitcoin and ether, so everyone wins!
🎬 MoviePass is back, and it starts at $10 / month
MoviePass is back, now with a points-based system that starts at $10 per month. Just be sure to use your credits, otherwise you'll end up with more unused credits than friends at the end of a free popcorn giveaway.
🤖 Google is starting to let users into its AI search experiment
Google is rolling out its Search Labs, offering a new experience with AI-generated summaries on top of search results. It's going to be interesting to see how users adapt to these changes and the impact it has on businesses and SEO. Hold tight while on the waitlist!
💀 YouTube Is Killing Stories
YouTube is killing off Stories, the ephemeral posts feature that few creators seemed to be using regularly. Existing stories will expire after seven days, and users won't be able to post new ones from June 26th. Instead, YouTube wants creators to use Community Posts and Shorts.
